## Before reporting a bug

Please include:

- extension version;
- browser and browser version;
- AI platform and page mode;
- exact reproduction steps;
- expected and actual behavior;
- redacted console output when useful.

Do **not** include passwords, cookies, access tokens, authentication headers, private conversation content, or screenshots containing confidential information.

For suspected security vulnerabilities, follow `SECURITY.md` instead of opening a public issue.
